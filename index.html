Citizens 4 Ontario

Making Ontario more transparent for all

For too long, critical decisions about Ontario’s neighborhoods, warehouse developments, infrastructure, and public budgets have happened behind closed doors. That ends when we start looking together.

Citizens 4 Ontario is a people-powered movement combining open-data investigation with grassroots organizing. We teach everyday residents how City Hall actually works, share what we uncover with every block in the city, and back bold progressive neighbors ready to run and win.
